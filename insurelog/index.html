Open, speak, done. Structure the conversation when you have a minute — goals, current cover, gaps, objections, next steps, and a clear disclaimer. A personal follow-up tool, not Applied Epic and not a quote marketplace.

Built for the solo agent day.

Every client keeps their conversations, policy lines, and renewals — so the next call doesn’t start from a scrap pad.

Inbox, then the right client

Dump the voice note first. Hang it on a Client when you know who it was — no forced form before you hit record.

Zones that stay honest

Goals, current cover, gaps, objections, and next steps — write by hand offline, or let optional AI draft a starting point. You confirm before anything is saved. Empty zones stay empty — never a silent “no gaps”.

Thin policies & renewals

Line up carrier, expiry, and a premium note. One tap can create a renewal task due 30 days before — you choose; nothing inserts silently.

Find it in seconds

Search clients, notes, and file names from Today. Jump straight back into the right conversation.

A memo you can share

Free: a light summary when you need something quick. Pro: a full needs memo with zones, policies, renewals, and a clear disclaimer — your notes, not a suitability report or application.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key (Gemini or OpenRouter). AI drafts; you edit and confirm. Nothing silent-saves behind your back. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same book on the devices you already carry. Sync only if you turn it on — your notes, your call. Freemium with a lifetime Pro unlock.

Yours, not a platform’s.

No InsureLog account to create. No company cloud holding your client files. Everything starts on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it talks only to the provider you pick — with your key. InsureLog is a personal follow-up workbench for independent agents, not an agency management system or marketplace.
