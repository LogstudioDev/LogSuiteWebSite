What you need between showings.

Buyers, sellers, and listings in one book — so matching inventory doesn’t mean digging through text threads.

Buyers, sellers & listings

Three ways in, one shared listing. Match homes to a buyer from Properties; open the same address from the seller’s card. Every showing stays findable both ways.

House facts ≠ buyer feelings

Condition and red flags live on the property across visits. Love / maybe / pass and next steps stay on that day’s showing — so you don’t mix “the roof” with “they hated the kitchen.”

Today’s next moves

Offers, second looks, listing prep — dated reminders on the person or the home. Optional Apple Calendar and Reminders when you want them.

Find it fast

Search people, notes, condition lines, and dates from Today. Jump straight back in.

Advisor memos you can share

Light summaries when you need something quick. Fuller property work-papers on Pro — framed as your advisor notes, never as a licensed home inspection.

Map when you need directions

Pin the address on the listing. Open Apple Maps. That’s it — no map-based home shopping, no heat maps.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key. AI can draft condition blocks and follow-ups; you edit and confirm. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same book on the devices you carry between showings. Sync only if you turn it on.

Yours, not a brokerage cloud’s.

No RealtyLog account. No RealtyLog business server. Notes start on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it only talks to the provider you choose — with your key. A personal showing sidecar — not MLS, not CRM, not a licensed inspection product.
