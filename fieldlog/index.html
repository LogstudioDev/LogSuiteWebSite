From the jobsite to a work paper — without a project cloud.

Open, speak, done. Structure the walk when you have a minute — progress, safety, punch, change $, and a clear disclaimer. A personal field tool, not Procore and not a signed CO.

  1. 01

    Record

    Hit record on the ladder, in the dust, or at the truck. When you stop, the audio is already safe on your phone.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Words become text in the background. Fix typos later. Speak the language you actually use on a walk.

  3. 03

    Hang it & deliver

    Attach the note to a Job Site and Contact. Organize punch and change estimates — then export a shareable site-walk work paper PDF.

Built for the solo GC walk.

Every job site keeps its walks, punch list, and change dollars — so the next visit doesn’t start from a scrap pad.

Inbox, then the right site

Dump the voice note first. Hang it on a Job Site and Contact when you know which job it was — no forced form before you hit record.

Walk zones that stick

Progress, safety, quality, materials, coordination, changes — write by hand offline, or let optional AI draft a starting point. You confirm before anything is saved.

Punch & change $

Line up closeout items and rough change estimates. Mark verbal approval on site when it happened — still a memo, not a signed CO.

Find it in seconds

Search sites, contacts, notes, and file names from Today. Jump straight back into the right job.

A work paper you can share

Free: a light summary when you need something quick. Pro: a full site-walk work paper with zones, punch/change $, photos, and a clear disclaimer — your notes, not a formal contractual change order.

Empty rows stay off the PDF

A bathroom remodel doesn’t need every catalog line. Untouched N/A items stay in the app; the exported work paper only prints what you marked or noted.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key (Gemini or OpenRouter). AI drafts; you edit and confirm. Nothing silent-saves behind your back. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same book on the devices you already carry. Sync only if you turn it on — your notes, your call. Freemium with a lifetime Pro unlock.

Yours, not a platform’s.

No FieldLog account to create. No company cloud holding your site walks. Everything starts on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it talks only to the provider you pick — with your key. FieldLog is a personal field workbench for solo GCs, not a project management network or formal CO system.
