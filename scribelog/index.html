What you need between patients.

Each patient keeps visits, SOAP, and return dates — so the next session doesn’t start from a blank sticky.

Inbox, then the right patient

Speak first. Hang the note on a patient when you know who it was — no chart picker blocking the mic.

SOAP without the EHR tax

Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan — hand-filled offline, or drafted with optional AI. You confirm before anything is saved.

Return dates that stick

Pin follow-ups on the patient. Optional Apple Calendar and Reminders — without living inside the clinic’s scheduling product.

Find it fast

Search patients, notes, SOAP fields, and dates from Today. Jump straight back in.

Share a clean PDF

Print or send the sections you choose — not a raw voice dump by accident. Private zip when you want your own archive.

One-tap from the Home Screen

Due Today for returns and tasks. Record to start a memo instantly — Lock Screen too.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key (Gemini or OpenRouter). AI drafts SOAP and follow-ups; you edit and confirm. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same patient book on the devices you carry. Sync only if you turn it on.

Yours, not a clinic cloud’s.

No ScribeLog account. No ScribeLog clinical server. Notes start on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it only talks to the provider you choose — with your key. A personal sidecar chart — not an EHR, not a covered-entity system.
