Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026
Last updated: August 12, 2026
App: ChartLog
Publisher: Logstudio, an independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”)

ChartLog is a personal clinical-memo app for clinicians using Apple devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and how optional system features work. ChartLog is designed to be local-first: the current product does not require a ChartLog account and does not use a ChartLog-operated business server to store your visit notes. ChartLog is not an electronic health record (EHR) and is not offered as a HIPAA-covered entity service.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the ChartLog apps published by us for Apple platforms (including iPhone, and where available iPad), and to this marketing website. It does not apply to third-party websites or apps you may open from links (for example, Apple’s App Store).

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to create a ChartLog user account.
  • Patient names, visit notes, transcripts, SOAP fields, reminders, to-dos, and related metadata are stored using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData), with optional sync through your iCloud private database (CloudKit).
  • Voice recordings are saved as files on your device and may sync via iCloud Documents when available.
  • Speech-to-text uses Apple’s Speech framework; depending on device settings and length of audio, processing may occur on-device or via Apple’s speech services under Apple’s terms.
  • Optional “Organize with AI” sends transcript text to the AI provider you configure, using an API key you supply that remains on your device.
  • Local notifications are scheduled on your device for reminders and dated to-dos.
  • If you enable sync, selected items may be written to Apple Calendar and/or Apple Reminders; turning sync off removes ChartLog-created copies from those apps.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information the app processes

3.1 Information you provide in the app

Depending on how you use ChartLog, you may enter or capture:

  • Patient information — such as name and optional demographics or notes you choose to store.
  • Voice recordings — audio you choose to record as visit memos.
  • Transcripts, SOAP, and notes — text generated from speech recognition, structured S/O/A/P fields, and any edits you make.
  • Reminders and to-dos — titles, due dates, and completion status.
  • Settings preferences — such as language UI choice, speech language, appearance, and whether to sync to Apple Calendar / Reminders.
  • Optional AI credentials — an API key and provider settings you enter; keys are stored in the device Keychain / local settings and are not uploaded to ChartLog servers.

Because ChartLog is built for clinicians, the content you choose to record may include sensitive health-related information. You control what you record and store. You are responsible for complying with applicable privacy, professional, and institutional obligations.

3.2 Information collected automatically

The current ChartLog app does not operate a ChartLog analytics backend that collects usage telemetry from your notes. Apple may collect standard App Store / operating-system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. This website may be served by GitHub Pages or similar static hosting; the host’s server logs may include IP address, browser type, and request timestamps as part of ordinary web hosting.

4. Device permissions

ChartLog may request the following permissions, only as needed for features you use:

  • Microphone — to record voice notes.
  • Speech Recognition — to transcribe recordings into text.
  • Notifications — to alert you about reminders and dated to-dos.
  • Calendars — optional; only if you enable Sync to Apple Calendar.
  • Reminders — optional; only if you enable Sync to Apple Reminders.

You can revoke permissions in iOS / iPadOS Settings. Some features will stop working without the related permission.

5. How information is used

On-device information is used solely to provide ChartLog’s features to you, including:

  • Saving and playing back recordings;
  • Transcribing and displaying notes;
  • Organizing notes under patients and an Inbox for unfiled items;
  • Storing and editing SOAP fields;
  • Scheduling local notifications;
  • Optionally creating or updating events/reminders in Apple Calendar / Reminders when you enable those switches;
  • Exporting a PDF or a private backup zip that you choose to create and share via the system share sheet;
  • If you use Organize with AI, sending transcript text to your chosen provider so it can return a draft for you to edit.

We do not use your clinical content for advertising.

6. Storage, sync, and retention

  • Local storage. Primary storage is on your device.
  • iCloud / CloudKit (text & metadata). When your Apple ID and iCloud are available, ChartLog may sync structured patient and visit data through your iCloud private database. If CloudKit is unavailable, the app falls back to local storage.
  • iCloud Documents (audio). Voice recordings may be stored under ChartLog’s iCloud Documents container (visible in the Files app) in addition to local Application Support storage.
  • Apple Calendar / Reminders. Copies written while sync is enabled live in those Apple apps. Turning a sync switch off removes ChartLog-created copies; events and reminders you created yourself are left alone.
  • Exports. PDF exports and backup zip files are created on your device at your request. You control where you save or send them. Backup zips can include full transcripts and media — treat them as confidential.
  • Deletion. Deleting a visit, patient, or related item in the app removes related local rows and files (and synced copies according to Apple’s sync behavior). Deleting the app removes local app data subject to Apple’s ordinary uninstall behavior.
  • Device backups. If you use Apple device backups, ChartLog data may be included under Apple’s control and your backup settings.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your visit notes with third parties for their marketing.

Limited sharing or processing may occur only as follows:

  • Apple. Speech recognition, notifications, Calendar, Reminders, iCloud / CloudKit / iCloud Documents, App Store distribution, and device OS services operate under Apple’s terms and privacy practices.
  • AI providers (optional). If you enable Organize with AI, transcript content is sent to the third-party provider you select (for example OpenAI-compatible endpoints). That processing is governed by that provider’s terms. ChartLog does not operate the model.
  • Service providers. Website hosting (for example GitHub Pages) may process ordinary server logs for this marketing site.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security — noting that for the app itself we typically do not hold your clinical content on our own business servers.

8. Children’s privacy

ChartLog is intended for clinicians and adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

The product is designed primarily for clinicians practicing in the United States. If you use ChartLog elsewhere, you understand that on-device processing and Apple services may involve infrastructure located in different countries according to Apple’s systems and your account region.

10. Security

We design ChartLog to keep sensitive content on your device. You should protect your device with a passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and OS updates. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You remain responsible for physical and access control of any device where health-related information is stored.

11. Your choices

  • Decline microphone or speech permission (recording/transcription features will be limited).
  • Disable notifications.
  • Keep Calendar / Reminders sync off.
  • Decline to use Organize with AI, or remove your API key.
  • Edit or delete patients, visits, reminders, and to-dos in the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data (subject to Apple backup and iCloud settings).

12. This website

The ChartLog marketing website provides product information and legal documents. It does not collect account registrations. Contact links (such as email) are voluntary. If you email us, we will use your message and address only to respond to your request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves (for example, if optional cloud features are introduced). We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be described in an app release note where appropriate.
