Leave court with the case already written down.

Between hearings, open the mic and talk. Notes hang on the matter — dates, files, and next steps stay with it. Built for solo and small-firm lawyers who move too fast for a practice suite.

From hallway to hearing — without opening a practice suite.

Speak while it’s fresh. Hang it on the matter when you have a minute. A personal case sidecar — not Clio in disguise.

  1. 01

    Record

    Hit record in the corridor. When you stop, the audio is already on your phone — even if a call interrupts.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Words become text in the background. Clean them up later. Speak how you actually talk.

  3. 03

    Hang it on the matter

    Attach the note to a case. Add hearing dates, to-dos, and files — so the next court day isn’t a scavenger hunt.

What you need between courts.

Each matter keeps the notes, dates, files, and follow-ups you actually use — without dragging a whole firm OS onto your phone.

Inbox, then the right case

Record first. Hang it on a matter when you know which one it was — no mandatory case picker before the mic.

Hearings & to-dos on the matter

Pin court dates and dated tasks where they belong. Optional Apple Calendar and Reminders — you’re not locked into a firm calendar product.

Case files nearby

PDFs, images, and common documents live on the matter. Preview, rename, share — without opening a separate DMS.

Find it fast

Search matters, notes, dates, tasks, and file names from Today. Jump straight back in.

Share a clean PDF

Print or send only the sections you check — not a dump of raw voice by accident. Private zip backup when you want your own archive.

One-tap from the Home Screen

Due Today for hearings and tasks. Record to start a memo instantly — Lock Screen too.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key. AI drafts a summary and fields; you edit and confirm. Nothing silent-saves.

iPhone, iPad & Mac

Same casebook on the devices you carry. Sync only if you turn it on.

Yours, not a firm cloud’s.

No MatterLog account. No MatterLog case server. Notes start on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it only talks to the provider you choose — with your key. Built for personal case notes, not multi-lawyer firm workflows.
