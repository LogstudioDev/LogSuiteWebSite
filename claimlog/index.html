Inbox, then the right claim

Dump the voice note first. Hang it on a Claim and Claimant when you know which file it was — no forced form before you hit record.

Zones that separate fact from story

Cause, structure, interior, contents, systems, mitigation, and claimant statements — write by hand offline, or let optional AI draft a starting point. You confirm before anything is saved.

Damage $ & follow-ups

Line up rough repair estimates and reinspection tasks. Numbers stay memo-grade — never invented unit prices, never a silent “no damage” fill.

Find it in seconds

Search claims, claimants, notes, and file names from Today. Jump straight back into the right loss.

A work paper you can share

Free: a light summary when you need something quick. Pro: a full loss work paper with zones, damage $, photos, and a clear disclaimer — your notes, not a formal appraisal or proof of loss.

Empty rows stay off the PDF

A water claim doesn’t need every catalog line. Untouched N/A items stay in the app; the exported work paper only prints what you marked or noted.

AI only if you want it

Use your own key (Gemini or OpenRouter). AI drafts; you edit and confirm. Nothing silent-saves behind your back. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same book on the devices you already carry. Sync only if you turn it on — your notes, your call. Freemium with a lifetime Pro unlock.

Yours, not a platform’s.

No ClaimLog account to create. No company cloud holding your loss files. Everything starts on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it talks only to the provider you pick — with your key. ClaimLog is a personal field workbench for independent adjusters, not a carrier claims network or estimating marketplace.
