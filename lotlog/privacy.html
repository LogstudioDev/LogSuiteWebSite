Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026
Last updated: August 12, 2026
App: LotLog
Publisher: Logstudio, an independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”)

LotLog is a personal inspection-memo app for auction buyers, independent dealers, and mobile inspectors using Apple devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and how optional system features work. LotLog is designed to be local-first: the current product does not require an LotLog account and does not use an LotLog-operated business server to store your inspection notes. LotLog is not a certified multi-point inspection platform, vehicle history product, or dealer management system.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the LotLog apps published by us for Apple platforms (including iPhone, and where available iPad), and to this marketing website. It does not apply to third-party websites or apps you may open from links (for example, Apple’s App Store).

2. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an LotLog user account.
  • Vehicle identifiers, inspection notes, transcripts, VIF fields, recon items, reminders, to-dos, and related metadata are stored using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData), with optional sync through your iCloud private database (CloudKit).
  • Voice recordings and evidence photos are saved as files on your device and may sync via iCloud Documents when available.
  • Speech-to-text uses Apple’s Speech framework; depending on device settings and length of audio, processing may occur on-device or via Apple’s speech services under Apple’s terms.
  • Optional “Organize with AI” sends transcript text to the AI provider you configure, using an API key you supply that remains on your device.
  • Local notifications are scheduled on your device for reminders and dated to-dos.
  • If you enable sync, selected items may be written to Apple Calendar and/or Apple Reminders; turning sync off removes LotLog-created copies from those apps.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information the app processes

3.1 Information you provide in the app

Depending on how you use LotLog, you may enter or capture:

  • Vehicle information — such as year / make / model, optional VIN, mileage, color, asking price, and notes you choose to store.
  • Voice recordings — audio you choose to record as inspection memos.
  • Transcripts, VIF, and notes — text generated from speech recognition, structured inspection fields, critical screens, recon lines, Max Bid, and any edits you make.
  • Photos and attachments — evidence images you attach to a vehicle or checklist finding.
  • Reminders and to-dos — titles, due dates, and completion status.
  • Settings preferences — such as language UI choice, speech language, appearance, and whether to sync to Apple Calendar / Reminders.
  • Optional AI credentials — an API key and provider settings you enter; keys are stored in the device Keychain / local settings and are not uploaded to LotLog servers.

Because LotLog is built for commercial vehicle inspection work, the content you choose to record may include business-sensitive information. You control what you record and store. You are responsible for complying with applicable privacy, disclosure, and professional obligations.

3.2 Information collected automatically

The current LotLog app does not operate an LotLog analytics backend that collects usage telemetry from your notes. Apple may collect standard App Store / operating-system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. This website may be served by GitHub Pages or similar static hosting; the host’s server logs may include IP address, browser type, and request timestamps as part of ordinary web hosting.

4. Device permissions

LotLog may request the following permissions, only as needed for features you use:

  • Microphone — to record voice notes.
  • Speech Recognition — to transcribe recordings into text.
  • Camera / Photos — optional; to capture or attach evidence images.
  • Notifications — to alert you about reminders and dated to-dos.
  • Calendars — optional; only if you enable Sync to Apple Calendar.
  • Reminders — optional; only if you enable Sync to Apple Reminders.

You can revoke permissions in iOS / iPadOS Settings. Some features will stop working without the related permission.

5. How information is used

On-device information is used solely to provide LotLog’s features to you, including:

  • Saving and playing back recordings;
  • Transcribing and displaying notes;
  • Organizing notes under vehicles and an Inbox for unfiled items;
  • Storing and editing VIF, critical screens, recon, and bid fields;
  • Scheduling local notifications;
  • Optionally creating or updating events/reminders in Apple Calendar / Reminders when you enable those switches;
  • Exporting a PDF or a private backup zip that you choose to create and share via the system share sheet;
  • If you use Organize with AI, sending transcript text to your chosen provider so it can return a draft for you to edit.

We do not use your inspection content for advertising.

6. Storage, sync, and retention

  • Local storage. Primary storage is on your device.
  • iCloud / CloudKit (text & metadata). When your Apple ID and iCloud are available, LotLog may sync structured vehicle and inspection data through your iCloud private database. If CloudKit is unavailable, the app falls back to local storage.
  • iCloud Documents (audio & attachments). Voice recordings and attachments may be stored under LotLog’s iCloud Documents container (visible in the Files app) in addition to local Application Support storage.
  • Apple Calendar / Reminders. Copies written while sync is enabled live in those Apple apps. Turning a sync switch off removes LotLog-created copies; events and reminders you created yourself are left alone.
  • Exports. PDF exports and backup zip files are created on your device at your request. You control where you save or send them. Backup zips can include full transcripts and media — treat them as confidential.
  • Deletion. Deleting an inspection, vehicle, or related item in the app removes related local rows and files (and synced copies according to Apple’s sync behavior). Deleting the app removes local app data subject to Apple’s ordinary uninstall behavior.
  • Device backups. If you use Apple device backups, LotLog data may be included under Apple’s control and your backup settings.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your inspection notes with third parties for their marketing.

Limited sharing or processing may occur only as follows:

  • Apple. Speech recognition, notifications, Calendar, Reminders, iCloud / CloudKit / iCloud Documents, App Store distribution, and device OS services operate under Apple’s terms and privacy practices.
  • AI providers (optional). If you enable Organize with AI, transcript content is sent to the third-party provider you select (for example OpenAI-compatible endpoints). That processing is governed by that provider’s terms. LotLog does not operate the model.
  • Service providers. Website hosting (for example GitHub Pages) may process ordinary server logs for this marketing site.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security — noting that for the app itself we typically do not hold your inspection content on our own business servers.

8. Children’s privacy

LotLog is intended for adult professionals in the used-vehicle trade. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

The product is designed primarily for inspectors and dealers working with auction and wholesale vehicles. If you use LotLog elsewhere, you understand that on-device processing and Apple services may involve infrastructure located in different countries according to Apple’s systems and your account region.

10. Security

We design LotLog to keep sensitive content on your device. You should protect your device with a passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and OS updates. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You remain responsible for physical and access control of any device where business inspection information is stored.

11. Your choices

  • Decline microphone or speech permission (recording/transcription features will be limited).
  • Disable notifications.
  • Keep Calendar / Reminders sync off.
  • Decline to use Organize with AI, or remove your API key.
  • Edit or delete vehicles, inspections, reminders, and to-dos in the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data (subject to Apple backup and iCloud settings).

12. This website

The LotLog marketing website provides product information and legal documents. It does not collect account registrations. Contact links (such as email) are voluntary. If you email us, we will use your message and address only to respond to your request.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves (for example, if optional cloud features are introduced). We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be described in an app release note where appropriate.
