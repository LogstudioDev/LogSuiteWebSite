Built for the two-minute look.

Every car keeps its looks, what it needs, and what you’d pay — so the next lane doesn’t start from a blank scrap pad.

Inbox, then the right car

Dump the voice note first. Hang it on a vehicle when you know which one it was — no forced form before you hit record.

Condition that sticks

Outside, mechanical, inside, road feel — write it by hand offline, or let optional AI draft a starting point. Big red flags (structure, flood, fire) stay honest: you confirm before anything is saved.

Recon dollars & a ceiling

Line up what to fix and what it might cost. Pin a max bid and dated reminders on that vehicle — so “I’ll remember” isn’t your only system.

Find it in seconds

A memo you can share

Free: a light summary when you need something quick. Pro: a full inspection work-paper with costs, bid ceiling, photos, and a clear disclaimer — dealer notes, not a certified multi-point report.

One-tap from the Home Screen

AI only if you want it

Use your own key (Gemini or OpenRouter). AI drafts; you edit and confirm. Nothing silent-saves behind your back. Keys stay on your phone.

iPhone & iPad

Same book on the devices you already carry. Sync only if you turn it on — your notes, your call.

Yours, not a platform’s.

No AutoLog account to create. No company cloud holding your lot notes. Everything starts on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it talks only to the provider you pick — with your key.
