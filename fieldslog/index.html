What you need on the job.

A personal notebook organized by site — not an owner renovation diary, and not a full jobsite ERP.

Today at a glance

Due and overdue follow-ups, plus owner changes that still need a chase — so the next stop starts with a short list, not a blank screen.

Sites, one notebook each

Every job in one list. Tap into Info, Walks, Changes, To-Dos, and Files — the same site, the same notes, every time.

Walks with proof

Quick capture that saves when you confirm. Attach photos from camera or album. Speak when your hands are dusty — transcription stays on your device.

Owner changes with $

Description, estimate dollars, verbal approval on site, and a simple status line. Export a PDF summary when you need to send it — tracking only, not a signed change order.

Follow-ups that get done

To-dos with who fixes it and by when. Today surfaces what’s due across every site. Optional notifications and Apple Reminders for dated items.

Board across jobs

Active sites, open tasks, unsent change dollars, and overdue counts at a glance. Open the lists underneath for detail — the top metrics stay put.

Contacts you reuse

Owners and contractors in one library — not retyped on every site. Filter by role, link people to a site or a note, dial from the contact detail.

AI only if you want it

Optional. Bring your own key. AI drafts structure from a walk note — you review before anything saves. Skip it and everything else still works.

iPhone & iPad

Same jobsite book on the devices you carry. Free to use. Sync only if you turn on optional iCloud — your notes, your call.

Yours, not a platform’s.

Free to use. No FieldsLog account, no in-app purchases, no FieldsLog cloud holding your jobsite notes. Everything starts on your phone; sync is optional. If you use AI, it talks only to the provider you pick — with your key, and only after you agree. FieldsLog is a personal jobsite notebook for remodel crews and site leads, not a formal change-order system, owner renovation diary, or project-management network.
